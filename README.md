# The Mystery City

Deep inside the former Soviet Union lies a city that does not appear on any modern map.

Official records claim the city was abandoned decades ago. Roads leading to it were destroyed, documents were classified, and almost everyone who knew what happened there disappeared.

But the city was never truly empty.

---

## The Beginning

You are a prisoner serving a long sentence.

One day, government officials offer you a deal:

> Enter the abandoned city.  
> Complete the mission.  
> Come back alive.  
> Earn your freedom.

You are sent in with a small group of other prisoners and soldiers.

Your official objective is simple: investigate the city and eliminate the dangerous creatures living inside it.

But nobody tells you where the creatures came from.

---

## The City

At first, the city looks as if everyone simply vanished.

Cars are left in the streets.

Food remains on tables.

Apartment doors are open.

An abandoned school still has books scattered across its classrooms.

There are evacuation posters everywhere, but many of them have been torn down.

Some buildings are completely destroyed.

Others look almost untouched.

And at night, strange noises come from underneath the city.

---

## The Laboratory

While exploring, the group discovers documents referring to a secret underground research program.

Scientists had been experimenting on humans and animals, trying to create organisms capable of surviving extreme environments, radiation, disease, and severe injuries.

The experiments worked.

Too well.

The creatures became stronger, more intelligent, and increasingly difficult to control.

Eventually, something escaped.

Then the entire city was sealed.

---

## What Really Happened

The government claimed there had been an industrial disaster.

That was a lie.

When containment failed, Soviet authorities decided the city could not be saved.

A powerful bomb was dropped on the area to destroy the laboratory, the creatures, and every witness.

Thousands of people died.

But the creatures survived.

And decades of radiation and isolation changed them even further.

---

## The Mission Changes

As the group travels deeper into the city, they realize their mission was never simply to kill the mutants.

Someone has recently returned to the laboratory.

Power has been restored in parts of the underground complex.

Security systems are activating.

New experiments are appearing.

The creatures the player encounters may not all be decades old.

Someone is continuing the research.

---

## The Mystery

Throughout the story, the player discovers conflicting evidence.

Some documents say the mutants caused the disaster.

Others suggest the scientists deliberately released them.

Some recordings claim the bombing happened before containment failed.

And several files mention a final experiment that was never recovered.

# Subject Zero

Nobody knows what it is.

Or whether it is still inside the city.

---

## The Player

Eventually, the player discovers something even more disturbing.

They were not chosen randomly.

Their prison records contain information connected to the original experiments.

The government did not send them into the city simply because they were expendable.

They sent them because they believed the city would react to them.

And somewhere beneath the ruins is the reason why.

---

## Story Direction

The story is intended to unfold gradually through exploration rather than explaining the full truth immediately.

The player first sees the consequences of the disaster, then slowly discovers what caused it through:

- abandoned buildings
- classified documents
- radio recordings
- survivor messages
- environmental clues
- underground facilities
- encounters with mutants
- government communications
- conflicting evidence about the disaster

The central mystery is not only **what happened to the city**, but also **why the player was sent there** and **what Subject Zero really is**.

---

## Planned Story Structure

The full story can be divided into several major chapters and locations:

1. **The Arrival** — Enter the restricted zone and reach the abandoned city.
2. **The Residential District** — Discover what happened to the civilians.
3. **The Hospital** — Find evidence of early experiments and failed evacuations.
4. **The Military Checkpoint** — Learn how the city was sealed and who ordered it.
5. **The Underground Laboratory** — Discover the true purpose of the experiments.
6. **The Bomb Crater** — Uncover evidence that contradicts the official timeline.
7. **Subject Zero** — Learn why the player was selected and what still remains beneath the city.

---

## Core Themes

- mystery
- survival horror
- Soviet-era secret research
- abandoned urban exploration
- government cover-ups
- mutants and failed experiments
- unreliable evidence
- hidden player identity
- gradual story discovery

The truth is buried beneath the city.

And someone does not want it found.
