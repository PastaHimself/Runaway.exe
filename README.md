# The Mystery City

You and a group of friends will explore an abandoned city full of mystery and uncover the truth.

## Setting

The city is located deep in Soviet territory. It was built in the 1900s, but then disappeared, never to be seen again.

## The Truth

There was a lab where the Soviets managed to create mutants and special creatures. The creatures escaped from the lab, so the Soviets dropped a bomb on the city, wiping out all of the residents.

Somehow, the mutants survived.

## Goal

You are a prisoner who has been tasked with hunting down the mutants.
